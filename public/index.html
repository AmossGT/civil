About RoadSathi

RoadSathi is a public infrastructure transparency platform that brings road project information, government tenders, contractor details, awarded contract values, and supporting documents together in one place.

It helps citizens understand who is responsible for public road projects, track available project information, access complaint channels, and find contact details to reach the relevant authorities and contractors.

Each record includes its available source documents and verification status to support transparency and public scrutiny.

RoadSathi aims to make public infrastructure information easier to access, understand, and scrutinise.
